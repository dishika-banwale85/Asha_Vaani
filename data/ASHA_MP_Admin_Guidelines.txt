=====================================================
NATIONAL HEALTH MISSION (NHM) & NVBDCP
OFFICIAL ASHA WORKER INCENTIVE GUIDELINES (UPDATED)
=====================================================

SECTION A: MALARIA INCENTIVES
- RDT & Blood Slide Testing: An ASHA worker receives Rs. 15 per slide or test for preparing blood slides or testing through Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT).
- Positive Pf Case Treatment: An ASHA worker receives Rs. 75 per positive case for providing complete treatment for RDT positive Plasmodium falciparum (Pf) cases.
- Radical Treatment (Pf & Pv): An ASHA worker receives incentives for providing complete radical treatment to positive Pf and Pv cases detected by a blood slide, strictly as per the official drug regime.
- Referral & Complete Treatment: Historically, there was an incentive of Rs. 300 for referring a case and ensuring complete treatment. NOTE: This Rs. 300 incentive is currently NOT in the updated official list and should be verified with the local medical officer.

SECTION B: LYMPHATIC FILARIASIS INCENTIVES
- Line Listing: An ASHA worker receives a one-time incentive of Rs. 200 for the line listing of lymphoedema and hydrocele cases in all areas of non-endemic and endemic districts.
- Mass Drug Administration (MDA): An ASHA worker receives Rs. 200 per day (for a maximum of three days) for conducting the annual Mass Drug Administration for Lymphatic Filariasis. This daily incentive covers the target of 50 houses and 250 persons.
